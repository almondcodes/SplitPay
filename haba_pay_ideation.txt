Excellent catch 👌 — this is critical because **M-Pesa STK push** can only be triggered **from your backend to a specific phone number**.

Here’s how it works for **PesaSplit**:

---

### 🔑 Two Possible Approaches

#### **Option A: Organizer triggers STK for everyone (less ideal)**

* Organizer creates bill.
* Backend sends STK Push to **all participant numbers** immediately.
* Participants get payment requests even if they haven’t confirmed/joined.

⚠️ Downsides:

* Unsolicited STK prompts (spammy).
* If wrong numbers were added, strangers get push requests.
* Bad UX → people feel forced.

---

#### **Option B: Participant triggers their own STK (best UX ✅)**

* Organizer creates bill + shares link.
* Participant opens link → sees their share.
* Participant clicks **“Pay Now”** → this action **tells the backend to initiate STK push to that specific phone number**.
* STK appears only when participant is ready.

✅ Benefits:

* User-controlled → no surprise prompts.
* Cleaner audit trail (participant action = STK push).
* Matches natural flow (“I decide to pay my share”).

---

### 🏆 **Recommended Flow for PesaSplit MVP**

👉 **The participant should trigger their own STK push.**

* Organizer only sets up the bill and tracks.
* Each participant, upon clicking **Pay Now**, triggers an STK push for themselves.
* Backend logs payment → updates bill status in real time.

---

* **Organizer = creates + tracks bill.**
* **Participant = triggers and completes their own payment.**

Excellent 👌 — the **share calculation** is the beating heart of PesaSplit. Let’s go through how it should work:

---

# 🔑 Share Calculation in PesaSplit

### **1. Equal Split (default, simplest)**

* Organizer enters **total amount** + **number of participants**.
* Formula:

  ```
  share_per_person = total_amount / number_of_participants
  ```
* Example:

  * Bill = **Ksh 6,000**
  * 4 participants → `6000 / 4 = 1500 each`

⚠️ **Handling remainders (cents or uneven splits):**

* Round down for everyone except the last participant, who pays the remainder.
* E.g., 1000 ÷ 3 = 333.33 → 333, 333, 334.

---

### **2. Custom Split (optional at MVP, but powerful later)**

Organizer can assign amounts manually. Useful when:

* Some people had extra (e.g., ordered more drinks).
* Someone is contributing less/more intentionally.

Workflow:

* Organizer enters each participant + their share.
* Backend checks:

  ```
  sum_of_shares == total_amount
  ```
* If mismatch, app shows error (“Your splits don’t add up”).

---

### **3. Hybrid Split (future enhancement)**

* Organizer sets **equal base split**.
* Then tweaks specific participants’ amounts.
* Example:

  * Dinner Ksh 6,000, 4 friends. Base = 1,500 each.
  * Adjust:

    * Alice had dessert → 1,700.
    * Brian only had soda → 1,300.
    * Carol & David → 1,500.

---

### **4. Group Contributions (like chama / goal-based)**

Instead of splitting a bill, sometimes it’s:

* Target: **Ksh 20,000 fundraiser**
* 10 members → app suggests 2,000 each.
* But people can contribute freely until target is met.

This mode works well for **fundraisers / group projects**.

---

# 🏆 MVP Recommendation for PesaSplit

For launch:

* **Start with Equal Split only** (90% of use cases: meals, drinks, events).
* Add **Custom Split** in the second iteration.
* Add **Fundraiser Mode** later (super powerful in Kenya).
